# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and receiving feedback professionally
- Focusing discussions on improving the project
- Showing consideration toward other contributors

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Abusive, insulting, or threatening language
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions
- Any behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from participating in the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer rather than discussing sensitive matters publicly.

All reports will be reviewed respectfully and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
